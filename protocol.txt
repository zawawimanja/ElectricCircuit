The IR Remote and IR Receiver are using a proprietary infrared communication protocol.
 It's not I2C, SPI, or UART. The IRremote library handles the details of encoding and decoding the IR signals.
The Adafruit 7-Segment Display is using I2C to communicate with the Arduino.


i2C - 7 segment display communicate with arduino
SDA (Serial Data): Carries the actual data being transmitted.
SCL (Serial Clock): Carries the clock signal that synchronizes the data transfer between devices.
